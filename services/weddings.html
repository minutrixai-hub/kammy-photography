Every Ritual, Every Family Smile

A Hyderabad wedding is never a single evening, and neither is our coverage. KAMMY PHOTOGRAPHY plans around your full calendar, from the turmeric laughter of the haldi and the intricate lines of the mehendi to the music and dancing of the sangeet, the sacred pheras, and the grand reception. Whether your celebration runs over one long day or stretches across three or four functions, we are there from the first morning prayer to the final farewell, so no moment slips by uncaptured.

We shoot in two voices at once. Our candid style follows the unscripted colour of the day, the cousin who cannot stop crying, the grandmother adjusting the bride's dupatta, the stolen glance between the couple during the pheras. Alongside it we give you the timeless traditional portraits that families treasure for generations, the posed groups, the elders blessing the couple, the formal frames that look just as right in an album thirty years from now.

Because so much happens at once across an Indian wedding, we work as a two-photographer team. While one of us is with the bride during her getting ready and the haldi, the other is capturing the baraat arriving and the groom's side in full celebration. This approach means the rituals and the quiet family moments are covered in parallel, and you never have to choose between the priest's mantras and your father's proud tears across the hall.

Over the years we have worked at venues right across Hyderabad and Telangana, from grand banquet halls in Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills to heritage palaces, farmhouse mandaps on the city's edge, and temple weddings in smaller towns. We know how the light falls in these spaces and how each community's customs flow, which lets us stay one step ahead. After the celebration, we deliver beautifully designed printed albums along with a private online gallery, so you can relive every frame and share it with family across India and abroad.
