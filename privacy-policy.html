Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 June 2026

KAMMY PHOTOGRAPHY ("we", "us" or "our") is a photography and videography studio based in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the rights you have. It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act).

Information We Collect

When you enquire about a shoot, make a booking or subscribe to our updates, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, event dates, venue details, billing information and any preferences you share with us. During an assignment we also create photographs and video recordings of you, your family and your guests, which may include images that qualify as personal data.

When you visit this website we may collect limited technical information such as your browser type, device and general location, gathered through standard logs and analytics. We do not knowingly collect data from children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, deliver the photography and videography services you have booked, process payments, share your final images and films, and keep you informed about your project. With your consent we may also send occasional updates about our work and offers. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us.

Use of Your Photographs

The photographs and films we create remain our original creative work and we retain copyright in them under the Copyright Act 1957, unless we agree otherwise in writing. We may wish to display selected images from your shoot in our portfolio, on this website and on our social media channels as samples of our work. We will always seek your consent before using recognisable images of you publicly, and you may ask us in writing not to use your images for promotion. Your delivered images are provided for your personal use, and we ask that our credit is retained where the work is shared publicly.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us deliver our work, such as album printers, editors, payment gateways and cloud storage providers, all of whom are expected to protect your data. We may disclose information where required to do so by law or by a competent authority.

Data Security and Retention

We follow reasonable security practices to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse, including secure storage and restricted access to project files. We retain your images and project records for as long as is needed to deliver your work and to maintain our portfolio and business records, after which they may be archived or deleted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate data, to withdraw consent and to request erasure of your data where it is no longer required. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies and similar technologies to help the site function and to understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

Grievance Officer

In line with the Information Technology Act 2000 and the DPDP Act 2023, any questions, concerns or complaints about your personal data may be sent to our Grievance Officer, Kaushik, at kaushik@kammyphotography.com or by post to Banjara Hills, Road No. 12, Hyderabad, Telangana 500034, India. We will acknowledge and address your concern in a timely manner.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the revised date shown above. This policy is governed by the laws of India, and any disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Hyderabad, Telangana.
